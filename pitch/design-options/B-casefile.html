§ 4 How it works

Before it is evidence, it is a draft.

1.Reply. Paste what they sent. Parley writes one calm answer about the kids — brief, neutral or firm — and saves both messages to the record.

2.Lens. Check your own draft first. Every phrase a judge would read badly is marked, with what to write instead.

3.Record. The dated log becomes one PDF for the lawyer.

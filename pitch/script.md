**SAY**
We're in the Skills track, and here's the skill: answering a provocation calmly. Sounds easy. Almost nobody can do it. When you're attacked, you reply fast, you defend yourself, you write a whole essay, or you fire back. What actually works in court is the opposite: short, factual, about the kids. Parley trains exactly that. Two minutes a day, real texts, on a 28-day path. You fail, you see why, you try again. And it shows: your score goes up.

## If the jury asks

- **"Was it really just the texts?"** No. In that New York case false reports to the police mattered too. But the judge quoted his texts in the decision, and courts in every state look at how parents talk to each other. *(L.W. v. J.U., N.Y. Sup. Ct., Westchester County, July 9 2024.)*
- **"Isn't this just ChatGPT?"** ChatGPT gives an answer and forgets it. Parley keeps the dated record, trains the skill every day, and our code checks every AI answer: a flagged phrase must really be in the text, and no times or amounts are made up.
- **"Is it legal advice?"** No, and we say so on every screen. We show how a message reads, not what to do in court.
- **"Why no free trial?"** Same as Zimran's apps: a cheap first period ($19.99 for 4 weeks), then the regular price. Cancel in one click, 14-day money back.
- **"Where does the $40 come from?"** Each step has a source: ad cost (Triple Whale), clicks (WordStream), quiz steps (Adapty), renewals (RevenueCat). The model is in the repo.

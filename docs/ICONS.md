# Icons: what each one means and where it is used

Every option a person picks, and every benefit they read, has its own drawn icon. A reused icon makes people read the
wrong thing (a heart for "one child", a split for TalkingParents), so one meaning gets one picture.

- **Sprites.** `src/icons.svg.html` is the original pastel-tile set; `src/icons-parley.svg.html` is the drawn
  one-per-meaning set, generated by `node scripts/build-icons.mjs` (edit the script, never the output). Both are inlined
  into `index.html` by the `inline-icons` plugin in `vite.config.ts`; the second reuses the first's tile gradients.
- **Use.** `<Icon id="kids-2" size={40} />` (`src/components/Icon.tsx`, ids typed in `IconId`).
- **Channels.** `ChannelIcon` keeps WhatsApp's real mark. Every other channel is its tile from 22 px up and a flat
  one-colour glyph below that (Compose, ChannelTag). OurFamilyWizard and TalkingParents are drawn as what they are
  (a calendar two parents share; a recorded call and chat with a lock), never their logos.
- **Sheet.** `docs/icons-sheet.png`: every icon at 40 and 96 px.

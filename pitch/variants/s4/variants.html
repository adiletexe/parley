Every parent knows to stay calm with their ex. Under attack, almost nobody can.

The skill we teach is replying to a provoking message briefly, calmly and only about the kids, so that if it is ever read out in court, it helps you.

Why adults keep failing

  • They reply fast, while still angry.
  • They defend themselves point by point.
  • They write an essay nobody asked for.
  • They fire back, and that is the line the judge quotes.

How Parley gets them there

Two minutes a day with texts that sound like a real ex. Pick the reply you'd send, see why it's good or bad, then write your own and get it graded.

28 days in four weeks: Brief & facts, Don't take the bait, Firm boundaries and Court-ready habits.

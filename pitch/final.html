The problem

One angry text can cost a parent time with their kids.

In a custody case, the texts between parents are read by the judge. Courts look at which parent keeps the peace for the kids. A text written in anger can cost parenting time, a say in decisions, or legal fees.

And the other parent knows it. They provoke, at 10 pm, again and again.

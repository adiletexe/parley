# Real cases: a parent's own messages, used against them in court

Researched 2026-11-15. "VERIFIED" means the opinion text itself was read (official court PDF unless noted).
Quotes below are copied from the opinions. Nothing here is from a blog snippet unless marked UNVERIFIED.

## Ranked for a 30-second, non-lawyer story

### 1. Crews v. Crews — Michigan Court of Appeals, No. 346440, Oct 10, 2019 (unpublished, per curiam) — VERIFIED
URL (official): https://www.courts.michigan.gov/siteassets/case-documents/uploads/OPINIONS/FINAL/COA/20191010_C346440_53_346440.OPN.PDF

**Story.** After the divorce the mother had the kids most of the time; the father had alternating weekends and Tuesdays, with shared legal custody. The court had repeatedly ordered the parents to talk only through Our Family Wizard, but the mother kept texting — the opinion calls them "irrational, angry, and concerning text messages." During the custody hearing she texted that she would keep the oldest child out of a summer program; the court cut her time to weekends. She then texted that she would not obey that order; the court suspended her parenting time entirely, later allowing only supervised visits. Another text, quoted in the opinion: "The wrath of God is coming to you swiftly … You all dont [sic] even realize that you are being used b[y] satan himself."

**Consequence.** Father went from alternating weekends + Tuesdays to **sole legal and primary physical custody**. Mother: **10 supervised visits**, then every other weekend plus Wednesdays. The appeals court said her "vindictive" texts about the summer program "belie[d]" her testimony.

**Statute/factor.** MCL 722.23(j) (willingness to "facilitate and encourage a close and continuing parent-child relationship") — found to **strongly favor** the father; 7 of 12 factors favored him. Clear-and-convincing standard, MCL 722.27(1)(c).

**Outcome.** **Affirmed.** Caveat for honesty: the texts were not the only cause — unsubstantiated abuse reports to police/CPS, withheld parenting time (6 times, conceded) and school absences mattered too. The texts are what triggered the two emergency orders.

### 2. Treutle v. Treutle — Michigan Court of Appeals, No. 369854, Sept 12, 2024 (unpublished) — VERIFIED
URL (official): https://www.courts.michigan.gov/siteassets/case-documents/uploads/OPINIONS/FINAL/COA/20240912_C369854_51_369854.OPN.PDF

**Story.** When the wife filed for divorce she asked for a personal protection order and attached screenshots of her husband's texts. In them, the opinion says, "he stated that he hoped something 'really bad' would happen to her, and he referred to her as a 'dumbass,' a 'goddam piece of garbage,' 'fucking worthless,' and 'lazy as shit.'" He admitted the messages "were made in an effort to hurt her emotionally." He kept sending "verbally and emotionally abusive messages" after a restraining order.

**Consequence.** **PPO granted, plus a civil restraining order** in the divorce case; an ex parte order gave her physical custody of the three children. After trial the judge nevertheless gave him joint physical custody week-on/week-off — and the Court of Appeals **vacated that 50/50 award** and sent it back (on procedural/best-interest grounds, citing the ex parte order entered "to protect a family from domestic violence").

**Statute/factor.** MCL 722.27(1)(c), MCL 722.23(d); MCR 3.207 ex parte orders. (Factor (j)/(k) analysis is not discussed in the opinion.)

**Outcome.** Affirmed in part, **vacated in part (his equal parenting time)**, remanded. Final custody after remand: UNKNOWN.

### 3. Beukema v. Labar — Michigan Court of Appeals, No. 340072, May 17, 2018 (unpublished, per curiam) — VERIFIED
URL (official): https://www.courts.michigan.gov/siteassets/case-documents/uploads/OPINIONS/FINAL/COA/20180517_C340072_55_340072.OPN.PDF

**Story.** Two parents sharing a daughter could not agree on anything — a doctor's referral alone generated "numerous e-mails." At the hearing, "numerous e-mails, text messages, and voice mail messages were admitted … that showed that defendant [the mother] perpetrated significant verbal abuse against plaintiff." She admitted she "called plaintiff names, berated him, insulted him, questioned his intelligence, and mocked his physical disfigurement." On one voicemail the daughter's voice could be heard in the background.

**Consequence.** Father awarded **sole legal custody**; parenting time revised to a five-day rotating schedule. (His request for $2,500+ in attorney fees was denied — no fee award.)

**Statute/factor.** MCL 722.23 factors (f) moral fitness, **(j)** facilitate the other parent's relationship, and (k) domestic violence all weighed for the father; the court tied (j) directly to her "pattern of demeaning conduct."

**Outcome.** **Affirmed.** Caveat: she also had a domestic-violence conviction for physically assaulting him and violated probation by emailing him.

### 4. Jennings v. Jennings — Indiana Court of Appeals, No. 25A-DC-00457, Oct 3, 2025 (opinion, Weissmann J.) — VERIFIED via mirror
URL read: https://hallapproved.com/in/cases/appeals/2025/10690220/ (full text mirror; official copy on public.courts.in.gov not retrieved — verify there before quoting on stage)

**Story.** The mother said the father sent her "bizarre and rambling messages," including one claiming a religious sect made the COVID vaccine to "depopulate" the world, and one warning "there will be a blackout of all communications … in the not too distant future," asking her to bring the kids to him "[w]hen this happens." The court found he could not "effectively communicate with Petitioner and co-parent."

**Consequence.** Mother: **sole legal custody** (from joint). Father: **up to 4 hours a week, supervised by a professional agency, at his own expense**; child support raised (new amount not stated; old decree $165/week). Mother's fee request denied.

**Statute.** Ind. Code §§ 31-17-2-21, 31-17-2-8, 31-17-4-2.

**Outcome.** **Affirmed.** Caveat: weakest link to "texts" — the findings rest mainly on his anti-vaccine/"fringe" medical views, failure to get a mental-health evaluation, and refusing supervised visits for a year. Use only if you frame it as "his messages were Exhibit A."

## Checked, but weaker or not text-based

- **Durbin v. Durbin, N.C. Supreme Court No. 78A24, Aug 22, 2025** — read via mirror https://hallapproved.com/nc/cases/supreme/2025/10658761/ (official PDF blocked by Cloudflare). "Escalating level of conflict and unwillingness or inability to communicate in a reasonable manner" can be a substantial change (N.C.G.S. § 50-13.7). Mother got **primary physical custody and final decision-making** on major decisions; Court of Appeals reversed, trial order reinstated. BUT the communications are **emails** (father "argued with both Plaintiff and PC in multiple emails"); no texts quoted. Good for "the law now says this", weak as a text story.
- **Aguilar v. Aguilar, Ariz. Ct. App. 1 CA-CV 19-0437-FC, June 18, 2020** (memorandum) — read via https://hallapproved.com/az/cases/appeals/2020/4762050/. Mother "sent harassing text messages while drinking" → **order of protection**; court found "emotional blackmail, as Mother's text messages showed"; father got **sole legal decision-making** and relocation, mother **supervised visits**, father awarded "a portion" of fees (no $). Affirmed. Alcohol abuse is the main driver; A.R.S. 25-403(A)(6) not cited.
- **In re Marriage of Turner, Iowa Ct. App. No. 24-1878, Sept 4, 2025** — VERIFIED (official PDF). "Voluminous record of hostile and unproductive text exchanges"; but **nobody lost custody**. Court kept joint custody, switched to week-on/week-off, and **ordered them to use a parenting coordinator and a co-parenting app "rather than text messages."** Affirmed; no fees. Useful as a one-liner: "a judge banned them from texting."
- **Diacos v. Ringwald, Ariz. Ct. App. 1 CA-CV 23-0717 FC, Jan 28, 2025** — trial court gave mother sole legal decision-making partly for father "sending disparaging text messages"; **vacated and remanded** on appeal. Do not use as a "cost them" case.

## "Friendly parent" statutes (exact text)

- **Florida Stat. § 61.13(3)(a)** (2025, read from flsenate.gov): "The demonstrated capacity and disposition of each parent to facilitate and encourage a close and continuing parent-child relationship, to honor the time-sharing schedule, and to be reasonable when changes are required." — https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/61.13
- **Arizona A.R.S. § 25-403(A)(6)** (read from azleg.gov): "Which parent is more likely to allow the child frequent, meaningful and continuing contact with the other parent. This paragraph does not apply if the court determines that a parent is acting in good faith to protect the child from witnessing an act of domestic violence or being a victim of domestic violence or child abuse." — https://www.azleg.gov/ars/25/00403.htm
- **Michigan MCL 722.23(j)**: "The willingness and ability of each of the parties to facilitate and encourage a close and continuing parent-child relationship between the child and the other parent or the child and the parents." — text as quoted verbatim in Crews and Beukema above; legislature.mi.gov refused the connection from here, so not checked on the legislature site itself.

## More cases with quoted texts

Researched 2026-11-15. All read in full text on CourtListener (opinion pages, from the court's own filed text) or on nycourts.gov/reporter, opened in a real browser. Quotes are copied character for character, including [brackets] and [sic] as the opinion prints them. "Messages" means the opinion's word. Where it doesn't say "text", that's flagged.

### A. L.W. v. J.U. — N.Y. Sup. Ct., Westchester County, 2024 NY Slip Op 50879(U), July 9, 2024 (Hyer, J.) — VERIFIED (trial-level decision, not an appeal)
URL: https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10003616/lw-v-ju/ (official copy on nycourts.gov/reporter not opened — URL not checked)
This is the "L.W. v. …" case the Blank Rome NYLJ piece refers to. **Both sides of the exchange are in the opinion:**
- Mother (paraphrased by the court): "Plaintiff informed him that it was unacceptable that she had not spoken to A.U. for two days." Father: "[L.W.], first of all I gave him the phone and told him earlier to call you when he wanted, so fuck you" (asked about it, he said "it sounded familiar because it has been a customary response of his").
- Mother (paraphrased): "asked that Defendant get A.U. to sleep in his own room and in his own bed." Father: "fuck off and stop sending me these bullshit messages, look in the mirror and don't tell me what you want to do in my home and try to create a narrative, was that you trying to engage in some semblance of productive coparenting"
- Mother's e-mails asking why the boy was not in school. Father: "fuck off"
- Father, Jan 21, 2023: "I will not coparent with you, get that straight, I will not be part of this sick thing you call being a parent"
- Father, Sept 28, 2021: "fuck you, if I don't talk to A.U. I will call the troopers and CPS again, your grave is dug, you have dug your own hole, it will be nice when we get to kick dirt on it and be done with you for good"
- Father, Mar 2, 2024: "the Judge will see through your lying sack of shit lawyer"
He testified "he is embarrassed at the language he used in these messages."
**Sender:** father, who at that point HAD temporary sole custody.
**Consequence:** mother given "immediate sole legal and physical custody"; father limited to "a maximum of two visits per week, no longer than two hours each," supervised by a court-appointed therapeutic supervisor "paid solely by Defendant"; his phone access with the child terminated; a family offense of harassment in the second degree found against him, with an order of protection for the mother and a 5-year one for the child (to July 9, 2029); firearms surrendered.
**Caveat:** also driven by repeated unfounded CPS/police welfare calls, a false sexual-abuse report, and withholding the child. Some messages may be emails (the opinion says "messages" and once "e-mails"). Trial court, so there's no appellate affirmance. Whether it was appealed: UNKNOWN.

### B. McGhee v. McGhee — Wisconsin Court of Appeals, No. 2022AP365, May 11, 2023 (per curiam, unpublished) — VERIFIED
URL: https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10111215/lacey-dawn-mcghee-v-ivan-j-mcghee-iii/
**Quoted texts (father to mother):** called her a "selfish cold hearted bitch"; "Fuck off cow I fucking hate you and I will never fucking forgive you"; glad it was over because he "won[']t have to deal with having sex with a smelly nasty girl anymore"; "this means war"; she "will look stupid in court"; she would "[e]nd up in jail." Mother: whenever she "message[s] him in regards to the kids, it always turns into something else. It turns into [Ivan] calling [her] names."
**Consequence:** mother got **sole legal custody** and primary placement. Father got every other weekend (he argued it was 12.5% of the time; he had asked for 36–50%). The trial court said "no one should be subjected to that type of language or behavior."
**Outcome:** **Affirmed.** **How much the texts mattered: they were the main reason.** The court said sole custody was "not in regards to [Ivan's] treatment of the children but his inability to discuss matters with [Lacey]." The GAL based the recommendation "on the text messages." He argued the texts weren't about the kids, and the court of appeals said they "are clearly related to the children." The cleanest "texts → lost custody" case found.

### C. Sheppard v. Reed — Iowa Court of Appeals, No. 21-0280, Nov 23, 2021 — VERIFIED
URL: https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/5300226/david-scott-sheppard-v-chelsea-renee-reed/
**Quoted text (mother to father):** after a temporary joint-care order she moved 165 miles away and "texted David that she did not intend to follow the court's order, telling him: 'No fuck you [D]avid' and 'fuck the judge and his order.'" Other texts (paraphrased by the court) said "she might harm herself or the children because she regretted having children." She also blocked him until 15 minutes before pickups.
**Consequence:** father got **physical care** of the child. Mother was ordered to pay **$559/month** child support and made solely responsible for transportation (from the temporary order).
**Outcome:** **Affirmed.** **Caveat:** the "critical factor" was her hostility and unwillingness to co-parent. Child-safety evidence (a bruise, a car seat, a gun comment) and both parents' domestic-violence history also mattered. The "fuck the judge" text is evidence of that hostility, not the sole cause.

### D. In re Marriage of Cowger — Iowa Court of Appeals, No. 22-1254, Oct 11, 2023 — VERIFIED
URL: https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/9432005/in-re-the-marriage-of-cowger/
**Exchange:** the father blamed the mother for a child's reading. "When Leslie presented Brad with reading assistance for that child and asked for Brad's help with paying for it, Brad responded 'that's fucking retarded' and advised, 'I'm not in on this so either u pay in full or take me to court.'" He also "berated Leslie in text messages" over two cavity fillings, and he wanted an "untainted" second opinion.
**Consequence:** joint legal custody and 50/50 → mother got **sole legal custody and physical care**. On appeal, the father's time was cut from equal to **every other weekend (Thu–Mon) plus 10 summer days**, and he was ordered to pay **$12,000** of her trial attorney fees (she had asked for $22,605), plus GAL fees, appellate fees and **$1,821.48/month** child support.
**Outcome:** **Affirmed as modified** (modified against him). **Caveat:** the court cites "hundreds of pages of written communications." This exchange is one example, alongside medical refusals and disparaging the mother in front of the kids. "Conflict … so intense that it's almost unbelievable."

### E. Stricker v. Creed — Pennsylvania Superior Court, No. 38 MDA 2025, Oct 21, 2025 (non-precedential) — VERIFIED
URL: https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10708144/stricker-b-v-creed-j/
**Quoted text (father to mother, "in pertinent part"):** ". . . Second of all your [sic] lucky I didn't destroy that phone you gave to [J.C.]! After finding out u had that [other man] around my fuckin son just you can get off. If I'd been in my IDGAF mood I would have broke in ur fkn aptmn & beat the shit out of u on general principal[.] . . . [J.C.] tells me bubba came over again I'm fukin coming for you." He also posted on Facebook from her account: "Sorry you scumbag piece of shit ain't not [sic] way I'm having my son round that fucking low life criminal fucking goon . . . as long as he's there [J.C.] won't be[.]"
**Consequence:** a **3-year protection-from-abuse order**, with **temporary sole legal and physical custody** to the mother and **no visitation** for the father.
**Outcome:** **Affirmed** (remanded only to restore boilerplate saying the custody part is temporary). **How much the texts mattered: they were nearly the whole case** — a text, a voicemail and a Facebook post. **Caveat:** he didn't show up to the hearing. It's a threat case, not ordinary co-parenting rudeness.

### F. In re Marriage of Nimrick — Iowa Court of Appeals, No. 24-1227, July 2, 2025 — VERIFIED
URL: https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10622013/in-re-marriage-of-nimrick/
**Quoted texts (father to mother, sent after trial "seemingly using a fake or falsified number"):** "You should feel guilty you stupid fucking whore shits definitely going to get worse for you now"; "Your karma is coming really soon now too you stupid whore"; "You piece of shit you're going to hell for all that you've done." Earlier texts called her "druggie azz bitch," "piece of shit," "dumbass." The court deliberately quoted them: "A paraphrase … would sanitize or minimize the language."
**Consequence:** mother got **sole legal custody** (and physical care). A **protective order** was folded into the decree, including a ban on derogatory posts. Father ordered to pay ~**$2,300** of her attorney fees (for the default litigation).
**Outcome:** **Affirmed as modified** (the speech ban was narrowed). **Caveat:** there was a domestic-abuse finding and in-person abuse, and he had a criminal record. The post-trial texts are what triggered the protective-order request.

### G. Mahbub v. Mahbub — Ohio Court of Appeals, 10th Dist., No. 24AP-586, 2025-Ohio-5867, Dec 31, 2025 — VERIFIED
URL: https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10767424/mahbub-v-mahbub/
**Quoted texts (husband/father to wife):** "Tomorrow, you get to watch me ruin someone's life that you love dearly - like you've ruined mine. … Everyday will be wors[e] than the last, I swear on it all"; she "deserve[d] to burn in hell for [her] sins"; "If you show up tomorrow to be "here for me" it's going to be a god damn shit show …"
**Consequence:** the trial court had given HIM sole custody. The appeals court **reversed and itself awarded sole custody to the mother** ("forthwith", exchanges at a police station), and reversed the child-support designation. "Mr. Mahbub's repeated threats against Ms. Son weigh heavily"; "the text messages, in conjunction with the 911 audio recording, cannot be ignored."
**Outcome:** **Reversed in her favour** — a rare remedy. **Caveat:** these are marriage-era texts, followed by an incident with a knife, plus alcohol relapses. It's a domestic-violence case more than a co-parenting case.

### Checked — no word-for-word texts, but good one-liners
- **Matter of Cameron ZZ. v. Ashton B., 2020 NY Slip Op 02842, 183 AD3d 1076 (3d Dept., May 14, 2020) — VERIFIED** (nycourts.gov/reporter/3dseries/2020/2020_02842.htm). The father "acknowledged that he had sent numerous text messages to the mother in which, among many other angry, profane and disparaging remarks, he called the mother insulting names, stated that he hoped she would go to jail, warned her that she would lose custody, and said that it was sad that the child had to call her his mother." He defended them on the stand: "I do feel strongly at times that, yes, she is those things that I described." Result: joint custody and equal time → **mother sole legal custody**; father cut to Tuesdays and most Saturdays during the school year. **Affirmed.** The texts are central, but the opinion paraphrases them.
- **Matter of Paul D. v. Margarita O., 2024 NY Slip Op 02499, 227 AD3d 439 (1st Dept., May 7, 2024) — VERIFIED** (nycourts.gov/reporter/3dseries/2024/2024_02499.htm). The full relevant text: "The parties' primary means of communication has been though a parenting app, which preserves users' texts. The mother's texts were frequently hostile and insulting." Father got sole legal and physical custody; **affirmed**. No messages quoted. The main drivers were the ACS removal and her missed visits.
- **In re Marriage of Campbell, Mo. Ct. App. W.D. No. WD87402, May 27, 2025** — the father texted the mother's sister that she was a "selfish, ungrateful person," a "piece of shit". His texts threatening to drag the case out "until she ran out of money" are paraphrased. The mother got custody; affirmed. Weak as a slide.
- **Stacy V. v. Frank B. (Cal. Ct. App. 2019)** — still UNVERIFIED. Only the Studicata summary was found; the full text wasn't found on CourtListener in this pass.
